About Sweet Cakes Bakery

Brief history about us

Sweet Cakes Bakery was founded in 2001 by Jake Wooder. It was established to bring sweet, rich, and freshly baked bread, pastries, and custom cakes to the local community of Greenwood, using Emma Wooder’s (Jake’s grandma) recipe, that was passed down to Jake Wooder when he was 15 years old.

Our Mission

To bring fresh aroma to the air, to bake high quality, fresh products daily while creating a warm welcoming experience for every customer. To bring back exciting memories that were forgotten as a child. To make you feel like a child again.

Our Vision

To become the community’s first choice for baked goods and custom celebration cakes at reasonable prices, and to also expand the business throughout the whole of South Africa.

Our Target

Local residents, families that are planning celebrations like weddings, birthdays, and many more. Most of all anyone with a sweet tooth.

SO COME JOIN US, AND ENJOY THE SWEETNESS OF LIFE THAT SWEET CAKES HAS TO PROVIDE!
