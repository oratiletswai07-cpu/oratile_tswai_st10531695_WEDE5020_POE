Introduction

Welcome to Sweet Cakes Bakery. This is where rich aromas of freshly baked goods come from. We're a bakery that provides everything you need from bread, cakes, sweet baked goodies, and many more. So come join us in our journey to discover the tastes that Sweet Cakes Bakery has to offer. These recipes have been passed down through the Wooder family, created by Emma Wooder the grandma of Jake Wooder, who is the founder of Sweet Cakes Bakery. These recipes are meant to tap into your heart and bring back memories that were stored away for a long time, the joy and the excitement you once felt as child every time your mom or grandma baked your favourites.
